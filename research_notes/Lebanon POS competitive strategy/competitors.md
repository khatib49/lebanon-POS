# Lebanon POS Competitor Landscape (retail + restaurants/cafés)

Research method note: done on 2026-11-24. This session's network blocked full-page fetches (WebFetch failed for changelog.foodics.com, voxire.pages.dev and www.xstak.com), so every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries, not from reading the full pages. Pricing and feature claims should be checked on the vendor pages before anyone relies on them. Most Lebanon-specific sources are **vendor marketing or affiliate listicles**, which are flagged where used.

## 1. Which Lebanese/local POS vendors exist?

### Takeaway
Lebanon has a dense field of local vendors. On the restaurant side there are long-established players (BIM POS, Omega POS) and newer cloud entrants (CeevTech, Voxire RTYLR). There are also ERP-with-POS houses (IDS Libra, MAPOS, Nexel, SBS, Wizard, Potech/MicroStep) and hardware-led resellers (POPs LEB / POSBANK). Several advertise USD/LBP dual currency, which has become standard for any Lebanon-focused product.

### Cited Findings
**Restaurant-centric local vendors**
- **BIM POS** (bimpos.com): based in Dora, Metn. Sells restaurant and retail POS plus ERP for inventory, purchasing and accounting. Also serves Gulf and African markets — [BIM POS](https://bimpos.com/); [search summary of Lebanese vendors](https://bimpos.com/). Runs an "exclusive agent in your region" reseller programme — [BIM POS careers/agents](https://bimpos.com/careers)
- BIM POS listed features: waitstaff management, delivery management, gift cards, inventory, loyalty, table management, split checks, online ordering. Capterra lists "Available Countries: Lebanon" — [Slashdot BIM POS](https://slashdot.org/software/p/BIM-POS/); [Capterra BIM POS reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/143097/BIM-POS/reviews/)
- **Omega POS / Omega Software SAL** (omegapos.com): located in Jisr El Wati, Beirut. Described as strong in hospitality, retail and POS — [Omega POS](https://www.omegapos.com/). Has a cloud restaurant product ("Cloud Based Restaurant Software Solution") with menu customization from the restaurant or from home, reporting, and automated purchasing/receiving — [Omega Cloud POS](https://www.omegapos.com:443/cloudpos); [Omega Cloud brochure](https://www.omegapos.com/Downloads/brochures/OmegaCloud.pdf). Has a free-to-download iPad app — [App Store Omega POS](https://apps.apple.com/tn/app/omega-pos/id1442087037?l=ar). Is hiring Beirut on-site support staff for its "restaurant and retail point-of-sale (POS) system" — [Omega job posting](https://omegapos.com/jobs/48)
- A competitor comparison table describes Omega as specialized for hospitality (kitchen display, digital menu) and "not best for retail". This is competitor-authored — [XStak blog](https://www.xstak.com/blog/best-pos-software-in-lebanon)
- **CeevTech** (Jbeil/Byblos, founded 2018): "CeevTech Restaurant ERP is a cloud POS built in Lebanon for restaurants, cafés and snacks". Covers tables, recipe costing, stock, delivery and loyalty. Supports both USD and LBP. Sold in Basic, Pro and Ultra plans, Arabic and English — [G2 CeevTech](https://www.g2.com/sellers/ceevtech); [Slashdot CeevTech ERP](https://slashdot.org/software/p/CeevTech-ERP/)
- **Voxire RTYLR**: a restaurant POS marketed for Lebanon with Arabic RTL, offline mode for power/internet outages, dual USD/LBP pricing with daily rate sync, and native Toters and Talabat integration. Self-promoting vendor blog — [Voxire best POS for Lebanese restaurants 2026](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/best-pos-systems-lebanese-restaurants-2026/); [Voxire RTYLR](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/restaurant-tech-lebanon-pos-rtylr/)

- **MAPOS** (I-MAD Technology, Beirut): ERP + POS + CRM with USD and LBP support and an offline till — [MAPOS](https://www.mapos.tech/en); [About MAPOS](https://www.mapos.tech/en/about)
- **Nexel ERP** (Nexel IT Solutions, Sanayeh, Beirut, founded 2023): shop and restaurant POS, stock and accounting in USD and LBP — [Nexel IT Solutions](https://nexelitsolutions.com/)

- **Wizard Cloud ERP** (Wizard Solutions SAL): cloud-based, handles Lebanese VAT reporting. **Potech / MicroStep "Socrate"**: reported as widely used by medium-to-large Lebanese firms. ### Inferences
- Dual USD/LBP support is offered by CeevTech, MAPOS, Nexel, POPs, RTYLR and Odoo add-ons, so it is now **table stakes** and no longer a differentiator.
- Market structure: (a) legacy on-premise restaurant suites (BIM, Omega) with reseller/agent networks, (b) ERP houses for which POS is one module (IDS, MAPOS, Nexel, SBS, Wizard, Potech), (c) newer cloud restaurant entrants (CeevTech, RTYLR), (d) hardware-led bundles (POPs/POSBANK), (e) Odoo integrators.
- Few, if any, local vendors appear to be mobile-first or self-serve with transparent pricing. That gap is the likely opening for a new entrant.

### Gaps
- No market-share data for any vendor. No independent count of installations.
- Could not confirm other names commonly cited informally (e.g., Aronium resellers, Microsoft RMS/Dynamics resellers, local Sage resellers) through search results in this session.
- LinkedIn headcounts and founding dates (except CeevTech 2018 and Nexel 2023) were not retrieved.

## 2. Which regional/international POS products are used or available in Lebanon?

### Takeaway
**Foodics** is the clearest regional player with confirmed Lebanon product work: in 2025 it shipped a Lebanon-only change that raises transaction limits because of LBP magnitudes. It also supports secondary-currency receipts. **Odoo POS** is available through several local partners. **Loyverse** is used as the free/cheap option, though I found no Lebanon-specific evidence for it. **Square and Toast** appear only in generic directory listicles, and their availability in Lebanon is **doubtful**. **Oracle Simphony** and **Sapaad** are present in MENA, but I found no Lebanese reseller.

### Cited Findings
- **Foodics**: its changelog post "Enhanced support for high-value orders in the Lebanese market" says the old transaction cap caused problems because at about 90,000 LBP/USD a routine ~$115 order exceeded the cap. The update "applies only to Lebanon". The post notes that Lebanese businesses often accept more than one currency. It is dated about mid-2025 ("6 months ago" on the changelog listing; exact date not captured) — [Foodics changelog](https://changelog.foodics.com/enhanced-support-for-high-value-orders-in-the-lebanese-market-3vkY0w)
- Foodics can print a secondary currency on receipts — [Foodics changelog via search summary](https://changelog.foodics.com/enhanced-support-for-high-value-orders-in-the-lebanese-market-3vkY0w)
- Foodics job listings name offices in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Jordan, Egypt and Kuwait. **Lebanon is not listed as an office location** — [Foodics on Workable](https://jobs.workable.com/companies/dNDv7kYXzuuC6vh18jDDQf.md)
- Foodics is the "Official Restaurant Management Technology Partner" of MENA's 50 Best Restaurants — [World's 50 Best MENA](https://www.theworlds50best.com/mena/en/partners/foodics.html)
- **Odoo POS**: several third-party dual-currency POS modules exist on the Odoo App Store (pos_dual_currency, star_pos_multi_currency, dps_aio_pos_dual_currency, for v14–v19). Some cost roughly $50–$90 one-off. LBP is not specifically named — [Odoo pos_dual_currency v19](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/pos_dual_currency); [star_pos_multi_currency](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/star_pos_multi_currency); [dps_aio_pos_dual_currency](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/dps_aio_pos_dual_currency)
- **Generic "top POS in Lebanon" directory** lists Toast, Lavu, Square, HDPOS Smart and Loyverse. This is a generic SEO listing and is **not evidence of local availability** — [SoftwareSuggest Lebanon](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/point-of-sale-pos-software/lebanon)
- Voxire (a competitor, self-interested) claims Square has gaps in local payments and in "the dollar-dominant pricing model", and that Loyverse suits a single café under $20K/month revenue — [Voxire](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/best-pos-systems-lebanese-restaurants-2026/)
- **Oracle Simphony**: cloud restaurant POS. Oracle says it serves 180+ countries and has Arabic Middle East pages, but no Lebanese partner is named. Partners are listed via Oracle Cloud Marketplace — [Oracle Simphony (ME Arabic)](https://www.oracle.com/middleeast-ar/food-beverage/micros/); [Oracle Simphony](https://www.oracle.com/industries/micros)
- **Sapaad**: cloud F&B POS, Singapore-founded, listed among MENA POS options. No Lebanon mention found — [Zyda: best POS in MENA](https://zyda.com/blog/9-of-the-best-pos-systems-in-the-mena-region); [Capterra Sapaad vs Simphony](https://www.capterra.ae/compare/143844/202814/sapaad/vs/oracle-micros-simphony)
- **XStak** (Pakistan/regional retail commerce vendor) publishes a "best POS software in Lebanon" blog, which suggests it is targeting the market — [XStak](https://www.xstak.com/blog/best-pos-software-in-lebanon)

### Inferences
- The Foodics Lebanon-only limit fix implies real Foodics customers in Lebanon (likely served remotely or through partners, since there is no Lebanon office). It is the most credible premium cloud competitor for restaurants and cafés.
- Square and Toast depend on their own card acquiring in a short list of countries (US/UK/Canada/Australia and similar), and Lebanon is not among them. They are **very unlikely to be usable in Lebanon**. This is not confirmed by a source retrieved here, so verify against their supported-country pages.
- Loyverse (free, app-store distributed, works offline) is probably the de facto free option for micro cafés and shops. Usage in Lebanon is unverified.
- Oracle MICROS/Simphony is likely limited to hotels and large chains. No local evidence was found.

### Gaps
- No confirmed Lebanese reseller for Oracle Simphony, Lightspeed, Revel, Sapaad, Microsoft Dynamics/RMS, QuickBooks POS or Sage. Their availability in Lebanon is **uncertain**.
- No confirmation of Square/Toast/Lightspeed supported-country lists (pages not retrievable in this session).
- Foodics Lebanon pricing and whether it has a local partner are unknown.

**VAT / regulatory context**
- Lebanon's standard VAT rate is 11%. Returns are quarterly, with the periodic declaration due within one month after quarter end under the 2026 Budget Law. **No general e-invoicing mandate is in force**, though electronic reporting has expanded for stamp-duty documents. Source is a third-party tax guide — [Kintsugi Lebanon VAT guide 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/middle-east/lebanon)
- From October 2025 the MoF reportedly requires tax and administrative documents (including VAT) to be submitted electronically. Unverified against an official MoF notice — [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/?p=331951)
- Government is moving tax/fee payments to bank cards and online. Online-only collection of certain fees was reported effective 1 Nov 2025 — [LBC](https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/news-bulletin-reports/878399/say-goodbye-to-cash-lebanon-to-allow-card-payments-for-taxes-and-fees/en); [Kataeb](https://en.kataeb.org/articles/lebanon-to-roll-out-bank-card-payments-for-taxes)

### Inferences
- With no fiscal-printer or e-invoicing certification regime found, regulatory compliance is not a moat for incumbents today. VAT-ready invoices (11%, TVA number, dual-currency totals) remain a must-have, and a future e-invoicing mandate would be a disruption point.
- The conflicting BIM POS figures ($29/month versus $500 one-time per user) suggest a mix of perpetual-licence and subscription models among incumbents. That mix is typical of older Lebanese vendors.

### Gaps
- No verified USD monthly prices for Omega, CeevTech, MAPOS, Nexel, IDS Libra, SBS, Foodics (Lebanon), or Odoo partner packages.
- Customer-credit ("dayn"/tab) support, hardware bundle prices, and support SLAs were not found for any vendor.

## 4. What do customers complain about?

### Takeaway
I found **no Lebanon-specific user complaints**: no Reddit r/lebanon threads surfaced, and Facebook groups cannot be searched with these tools. Review-site data for BIM POS is small, old and uniformly positive. Global Loyverse reviews flag weak accounting, missing correction/audit controls and free-tier limits. Lebanon-specific pain points (outages, rate changes, cash) are documented through vendor content and news about dollarization rather than direct reviews.

### Cited Findings
- BIM POS on Capterra: 46 reviews, 100% positive sentiment, value-for-money 4.9 from 19 ratings. **Most reviews are dated 2015–2018**, and one GetApp review is from 2016. The sample is likely skewed — [Capterra BIM POS](https://www.capterra.com/p/143097/BIM-POS/reviews/); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/retail-consumer-services-software/a/bim-pos)
- Loyverse (global reviews): weak accounting integration; iOS hardware limits that add cost; a wrong payment type cannot be corrected; open tickets can be deleted without an audit trail; free plan cap reported at 100 orders, after which a subscription is needed. Ratings are 3.8/5 (MobileTransaction), 4.05/5 (Fit Small Business) and about 4.5 (Capterra) — [Capterra Loyverse](https://www.capterra.com/p/150632/Loyverse-POS/reviews); [MobileTransaction](https://mobiletransaction.org/loyverse-review); [Fit Small Business](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/loyverse-review/)
- Vendor claims about Lebanese needs (self-interested): cash is 40–60% of restaurant transactions; "the dollar-LBP dynamic means dual-pricing logic is required"; offline mode is essential because of power and internet cuts — [Voxire](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/best-pos-systems-lebanese-restaurants-2026/)
- Foodics had to raise transaction caps for Lebanon because LBP-denominated totals were rejected. This is direct evidence that **imported POS products break on LBP magnitudes** — [Foodics changelog](https://changelog.foodics.com/enhanced-support-for-high-value-orders-in-the-lebanese-market-3vkY0w)

### Inferences
- Likely pain points a new entrant can attack: opaque "call us" pricing, perpetual-licence plus per-user costs, on-premise installs that need a technician, imported tools mishandling LBP amounts and exchange rates, and lack of audit trails for staff fraud. These come from the findings above rather than from direct Lebanese reviews.

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/lebanon, Facebook group, Google Play Lebanon, or Lebanese media (Executive Magazine, L'Orient Today, The961, Wamda) complaints about specific POS vendors were found in this session. This should be filled by manual Facebook-group and Google Maps review research.

## 5. Are cheap or informal options dominant among small shops?

### Takeaway
I found no quantitative data on how many Lebanese small shops use notebooks, Excel, pirated software or WhatsApp ordering. Context strongly suggests a large informal, cash-based segment where shops reprice often in USD. Free apps like Loyverse are the main "cheap" formal alternative.

### Cited Findings
- Dollarization spread because businesses and individuals refused a currency that kept losing value, not by decree. A Beirut shop owner described hourly exchange-rate hits before switching to dollar pricing — [Khaleej Times / AP 2023](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/lebanon-adopts-dollarization-as-currency-economy-crumble?amp=1) (**2023, older**)
- Small businesses risked "selling products at a loss just minutes after stacking them on the shelves" during the currency collapse — [inkl / news](https://www.inkl.com/news/small-businesses-in-lebanon-struggle-to-stay-afloat-amid-lira-s-endless-fall) (older, about 2021–2023)
- Exchange rates are tracked informally through WhatsApp and Telegram groups (money changers) — [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1344488/lebanon-runs-on-informal-money-exchangers-whats-it-like-to-be-one.html)
- Small family businesses commonly operate in cash and rely on diaspora transfers — [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/lebanon-dollar-replaced-disappearing-lira-how)
- Loyverse is a free POS; reviewers valued its offline operation — [Capterra Loyverse](https://www.capterra.com/p/150632/Loyverse-POS/reviews)

### Inferences
- The real "competitor" for micro-shops is probably the notebook, Excel, or a free app, plus WhatsApp for orders and exchange rates. A new entrant must beat zero cost with a very low price, offline-first design, and LBP/USD rate handling that saves daily repricing work.
- Since the LBP rate has been roughly stable near 89,500/USD since 2023, the pain has shifted from hourly repricing to dual-currency cash handling and change-giving. This is an inference and was not sourced in this session.

### Gaps
- No survey or statistic on POS adoption rate, pirated software usage, or WhatsApp ordering among Lebanese SMEs. No Lebanese fintech/POS-terminal data (e.g., Areeba, bank terminals) was retrieved.
- Network restriction: changelog.foodics.com, voxire.pages.dev and www.xstak.com could not be fetched (hosts must be allowed in session network settings to read full pages).
