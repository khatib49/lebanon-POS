# Lebanon POS: Market Size, Willingness to Pay, Go-to-Market and Pricing

Research notes compiled 2026-11-24. Method: web search (about 18 queries). Direct page fetches to some hosts (thebeiruter.com, foodics.com) were blocked by the session network, so several figures come from search-result snippets of the cited pages and should be spot-checked before publication. MENA/emerging-market benchmarks are labeled as such.

## 1. Number of businesses in Lebanon and post-crisis trends

### Takeaway
No current official census of Lebanese establishments exists. The usable anchors come from the restaurant syndicate (about 8,500 F&B outlets in 2019, about 5,500 in 2025, plus 402 new brands registered in 2025), roughly 3,200 licensed pharmacies (2018), and a highly fragmented grocery sector with only about 120 true supermarkets. A strong 2025 rebound (+44.6% visitors, GDP +4.2%) was undone by the March 2026 escalation, and the World Bank now projects GDP -6.4% for 2026. Treat 2026–27 as a volatile, recovery-dependent market.

### Cited Findings
**SMEs overall (older data)**
- SMEs are about 95% of Lebanese enterprises under the Ministry of Economy's proposed definition (2014, older figure). — [MoET SME Strategy launch, 2014](https://www.economy.gov.lb/media/10311/launching-of-the-sme-strategy-december-16-2014.pdf)
- Micro enterprises are about 73% of SMEs (2018, older figure). — [MoET, SMEs in Lebanon 2018](https://www.economy.gov.lb/media/11222/smes-in-lebanon-180412-19-website.pdf)
- CAS has no recent commerce-establishment census. BLOM's BRITE series of new trade establishments from the Business Register (CAS / Ministry of Justice) only covers 2011–2014. — [BLOMInvest BRITE](https://brite.blominvestbank.com/series/New-Establishments-Trade-and-Auto-Repair-39953/)
- Industrial counts conflict: a private directory listed 18,542 facilities in 2024, of which about 8,771 were field-verified, while the Ministry of Industry estimates fewer than 7,000 registered industrialists. This shows how unreliable registry counts are. — [L'Orient Today, 2024](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1453152/how-many-manufacturers-are-there-in-lebanon.html)

**Restaurants, cafés, bars (Syndicate of Owners of Restaurants, Cafés, Night-Clubs & Pastries, president Tony Ramy)**
- 2019 baseline: about 8,500 restaurant establishments, per Ramy. — [LBCI](https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/press/693830/lebanons-restaurant-institutions-have-decreased-in-number-since-2019-r/en)
- Crisis closures: 265+ F&B outlets closed in Sept–Nov 2019 — [Beiruting](https://www.beiruting.com/news/11202/265-restaurants-closed-in-past-2-months-in-lebanon). 785 restaurants and cafés closed in about 5 months (to Feb 2020), with 25,000 jobs lost — [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/node/2140916); [Kataeb](https://en.kataeb.org/articles/economics-2020-02-18-syndicate-announces-that-785-restaurants-closed-in-past-5)
- 2023–24 "support front" war: Ramy reported a 90% fall in sector activity and hundreds of closures, temporary or permanent, especially in Downtown Beirut. — [This Is Beirut](https://thisisbeirut.com.lb/articles/1300245/who-will-save-the-restaurant-sector-from-the-cost-of-the-support-front)
- 2025: Ramy cited "5,500 restaurants, cafés, and nightlife venues" and said 402 new brands were registered at the Ministry of Economy between early 2025 and late summer. — [The Beiruter](https://www.thebeiruter.com/article/surviving-the-storm-inside-lebanon%E2%80%99s-resilient-restaurant-scene/566) (snippet; full page could not be fetched)
- Summer season kickoff: 50 new restaurants and 5 global franchise brands were announced. The article year is uncertain (2024 or 2025). — [Hospitality News](https://www.hospitalitynewsmag.com/syndicate-restaurants-gathering-summer-season/)
- 2025 global entrants included Eataly and Zara's first café in Beirut. One operator said "the number of establishments now exceeds demand." — [The Beiruter](https://www.thebeiruter.com/article/surviving-the-storm-inside-lebanon%E2%80%99s-resilient-restaurant-scene/566); [This Is Beirut, Summer 2025](https://thisisbeirut.com.lb/articles/1314339/sun-on-the-horizon-rough-skies-ahead-summer-2025-takes-off)
- The syndicate declared full readiness for summer 2025. — [Digital Star, June 2025](https://dstar.news/2025/06/11/lebanons-hospitality-sector/)

**Retail / supermarkets / dekkenes**
- Nabil Fahed (Syndicate of Supermarkets) said about 50 companies own about 120 supermarkets (undated). The syndicate has shrunk from about 50 members in the early 1990s to about 12. — [Hospitality News](https://www.hospitalitynewsmag.com/the-online-supermarket-sector-not-so-super/); [Business News (undated)](https://www.businessnews.com.lb/cms/Story/StoryDetails/4330/~/images/bar.jpg)
- Super/hypermarkets and malls hold about 40% of grocery retail share. The remaining ~60% is traditional and mini-market trade, i.e. the dekkene long tail. — [Catalonia Trade Portal](https://tradeportal.accio.gencat.cat/ca/mercats-potencials/liban/distribucio)
- I found no count of mini-markets or dekkenes (see Gaps).

**Pharmacies**
- 3,198 licensed pharmacies and 8,855 registered pharmacists (Order of Pharmacists data to 2018). Mount Lebanon has 1,366 and Beirut 235. — [Human Resources for Health, 2019](https://human-resources-health.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12960-019-0386-0/tables/5)
- MoPH MediTrack drug-tracking rollout references about 3,800 pharmacies to be linked, with more than 3,000 community pharmacies (undated MoPH page). This is a regulatory track-and-trace driver that a pharmacy POS would need to integrate with. — [MoPH](https://www.moph.gov.lb/en/Drugs/index/0/73235/page:14/sort:Drug.atc/direction:asc/letter:A)

**Macro, tourism, war**
- 2024 war (Oct 2023–Dec 2024): World Bank RDNA puts total cost at about $14B ($6.8B damage, $7.2B losses). Commerce, Industry & Tourism damage was about $0.6B (9% of damage). Reconstruction needs are about $11B, and private investment of $6–8B is expected to rebuild businesses and tourism. — [World Bank press release, Mar 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/03/07/lebanon-s-recovery-and-reconstruction-needs-estimated-at-us-11-billion)
- Tourism: 2024 arrivals were 1,131,100, down 32.1% from 1,666,492 in 2023 — [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1448628/lebanons-tourist-arrivals-drop-32-percent-in-2024.html). 2025 arrivals were 1,635,490, up 44.59% YoY (Ministry of Tourism data via BLOM) — [BLOMInvest](https://blog.blominvestbank.com/tourism-activity-rose-by-44-59-yoy-in-2025/). The definitions (total vs. foreign) may differ between years.
- July 2025 BEY airport: 873,095 passengers, up 16.25% YoY, mostly diaspora. Beirut hotel occupancy was 80–90% in summer 2025, and regional areas were above 60%. — [The Beiruter](https://www.thebeiruter.com/article/surviving-the-storm-inside-lebanon%E2%80%99s-resilient-restaurant-scene/566); [TravelMole](https://www.travelmole.com/news/lebanons-tourism-rebounds-summer-2025)
- World Bank Summer 2026 Lebanon Economic Monitor: real GDP grew about 4.2% in 2025 and is projected to fall 6.4% in 2026 after the March 2026 escalation, driven by a tourism collapse and weaker consumption. Inflation is forecast at 17.5%. — [Al Jazeera, Aug 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/world-bank-projects-war-hit-lebanons-economy-to-contract-by-6-4-percent); [Libnanews](https://en.libnanews.com/lebanon-war-plunges-economy-into-recession-after-technical-rebound-of-2025/)
- HORECA Lebanon 2025 (Apr 8–11, Seaside Arena) had about 350 exhibitors and about 24,000 trade visitors, up from about 160 exhibitors and 12,000 visitors in 2024, a sign of sector re-investment in 2025. — [Hospitality News](https://www.hospitalitynewsmag.com/?p=47728); [JETRO J-messe 2024](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/database/j-messe/tradefair/detail/131227)

### Inferences
- Serviceable segments (rough, to be validated):
  - F&B: about 5,500 outlets, plus churn of several hundred new brands a year.
  - Pharmacies: about 3,000–3,800.
  - Modern grocery: about 120 supermarkets.
  - Long tail of mini-markets, dekkenes and specialty retail: likely tens of thousands of shops, but there is no hard number.
  - A realistic paid-POS addressable base is probably around 15,000–30,000 outlets.
- F&B churn is high: closures in every shock and hundreds of new brands in recovery years. New openings are the best acquisition moment, because a POS is bought at fit-out.
- Demand is event-driven: summer and diaspora seasons, and ceasefire periods. Plan sales capacity and cash flow for stop-start years like 2026.

### Gaps
- No CAS or Ministry of Economy count of retail shops, mini-markets or total SMEs since about 2018. The Chamber of Commerce (CCIA-BML) membership figures were not found.
- No 2026 restaurant closure count after the March 2026 escalation.
- No current pharmacy count from the Order of Pharmacists.
- The Beiruter and Foodics pages could not be fetched directly (network block), so those quotes come from search snippets.

## 2. Willingness and ability to pay for software

### Takeaway
Lebanese SMEs operate in a dollarized, largely cash economy and have historically been heavy users of pirated software (69% piracy rate in 2017). Price sensitivity is high, and pricing in fresh USD with cash, Whish/OMT or fresh-dollar card collection is a practical must. I found no survey of monthly SME software budgets.

### Cited Findings
- The economy is largely dollarized and most payments moved to cash. "Fresh dollar" cards linked to accounts have worked again since 2022. — [US Commercial Service, Lebanon Country Commercial Guide](https://trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/lebanon-trade-financing)
- The World Bank estimated the dollar cash economy at about $9.9B in 2022, nearly half of GDP. — [LBCI](https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/news-bulletin-reports/721888/lebanons-cash-economy-challenges-and-efforts-to-reform/en)
- Card payments are surging, by +130% per BDL. Fresh-dollar check value doubled to more than $1B. BDL and Mastercard announced a $100k programme to expand SME digital payments, and Mastercard is building SME card-acceptance solutions. — [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1547050/card-payments-surge-130-in-lebanon-bdl-outlines-measures-to-curb-cash-economy.html); [Business News](https://www.businessnews.com.lb/cms/Story/StoryDetails/12469/Fresh-dollar-checks-tripled-to-$1-billion)
- The economy "still runs on dollars, largely outside the banking system." — [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1551032/lebanons-economy-still-runs-on-dollars-largely-outside-the-banking-system.html)
- In July 2025 BDL hired K2 Integrity to classify the cash economy by risk. This is an early AML/formalization push. — [LBCI](https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/news-bulletin-reports/868087/lebanons-banking-future-bdl-enlists-us-firm-to-tackle-unregulated-econ/en)
- Software piracy (BSA):
  - 69% in 2017, 37th globally and 6th among Arab countries, against a 37% global rate and 56% for MEA — [Byblos Bank](https://byblosbank.com/common/economic-research-new/lebanon-this-week/lebanon-this-week-540/lebanons-piracy-rate-at-69-in-2017-37th-highest-globally-and-sixth-highest-among-arab-countries)
  - 70% in 2015 and 72% in 2009 — [Al Bawaba](https://www.albawaba.net/business/lebanons-software-piracy-rate-high-chinas-us-report-846208)
  - IIPA notes that end-user piracy is "particularly" prevalent in SMEs (older, 2000s) — [IIPA](https://iipa.org/files/uploads/2018/01/rbc20102010SPEC301LEBANON.pdf)
- Inflation was over 45% in 2024 and is forecast at 17.5% for 2026, which squeezes margins. — [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1547050/card-payments-surge-130-in-lebanon-bdl-outlines-measures-to-curb-cash-economy.html); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/22/world-bank-projects-war-hit-lebanons-economy-to-contract-by-6-4-percent)
- The local market has a low-cost one-time option: OLX listings advertise POS systems "with free software" from $199, sold as a hardware bundle. — [OLX Lebanon](https://www.olx.com.lb/q-new-po/)

### Inferences
- The anchor competitors are "free" pirated or cracked desktop POS and the dealer bundle with bundled software. A SaaS product has to justify a recurring fee with things a cracked copy cannot do:
  - cloud backup (data loss from fires, blasts and power cuts is a real fear)
  - owner remote dashboard on WhatsApp/mobile
  - delivery-app integration (Toters etc.)
  - LBP/USD dual-currency pricing with a daily rate
  - support
- Collection matters as much as price. Offer USD cash via the reseller, Whish/OMT transfer, and fresh-dollar card. Annual prepay discounts help reduce collection overhead.
- Because of trust deficits after the bank collapse, many owners will prefer a one-time license plus an optional yearly support/cloud fee, or an offline-first design, over pure SaaS. A hybrid (perpetual local license plus a paid cloud add-on) is worth testing.

### Gaps
- No survey data on Lebanese SME monthly software budgets or license vs. subscription preference.
- No post-2017 piracy figure. BSA stopped publishing the global survey after 2018.
- No Whish/OMT merchant-payment adoption data found.

## 3. How POS/ERP vendors sell in Lebanon

### Takeaway
The dominant Lebanese model is channel-led: hardware dealers and resellers bundle software, and local vendors such as BIM POS use exclusive regional agents and franchise partners, then export through the diaspora. HORECA Lebanon is the key F&B trade show. I found no hard evidence on accountants as a channel or on the performance of social-media-led sales.

### Cited Findings
- BIM POS (Lebanon, founded 2008, Bouchrieh) claims 4,000+ installations in 23+ countries. Its markets include the GCC, Syria, West Africa (Ghana, Nigeria, Ivory Coast, Gabon, Togo), Armenia, Europe and the US. Listed starting price is $29 (Capterra; "contact vendor"). — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/143097/BIM-POS/); [TrustRadius](https://tr-web.dev.trustradius.com/products/bim-pos-masterdine/details)
- BIM POS franchise/agent model: partners get the software free for service ticketing, a branded site and their own reseller channel in their territory. In return they must hire sales and 24/7 support staff, keep an office of at least 60 m² in the main city, and sell POS hardware and networking. — [BIM POS franchise partner](https://bimpos.com/bim-pos-franchise-partner); [Elioplus channel profile](https://elioplus.com/profiles/channel-partners/48498/bim-pos)
- BIM POS actively recruits resellers in Ghana, which shows Lebanese-diaspora Africa as an export channel. It used Expo 2020 Dubai and a Ramadan event in Lebanon in April 2024 to launch QR menus. — [BIM POS Ghana](https://bimpos.com/gh/blog/the-dubai-expo-2020-as-the-largest-exhibitions-that-gathers-the-world)
- CeevTech (Jbeil) sells a cloud restaurant ERP/POS for restaurants, cafés and snacks. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/sellers/ceevtech); [Slashdot](https://slashdot.org/software/p/CeevTech-ERP/)
- A vendor-authored Lebanon guide says standalone POS plans start "under $40/month per terminal" and a full commerce suite at $99/month per branch. It says Square ($60/mo + 2.6% + $0.10) lacks local card support. Biased source: the vendor sells RTYLR. — [Voxire blog](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/best-pos-systems-lebanese-restaurants-2026/)
- HORECA Lebanon 2025: about 350 exhibitors and about 24,000 trade visitors, run by Hospitality Services. It is the main F&B buyer gathering. — [Hospitality News](https://www.hospitalitynewsmag.com/?p=47728); [L'Orient Today](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1455304/horeca-lebanon-opens-its-doors-with-an-increased-number-of-exhibitors.html)
- Foodics (KSA, MENA benchmark) uses financing as a sales lever: a two-year commitment with micro-installments and no upfront payment. Foodics has no free plan. — [Foodics flex program](https://foodics.com/flex-program-lp); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/foodics/pricing)

### Inferences
- Recommended mix:
  - Hardware dealers and IT resellers in each region (Beirut, Metn, Kesrouan/Jbeil, North, South, Bekaa), paid 20–30% recurring revenue share and responsible for install and cash collection.
  - A HORECA Lebanon booth for F&B.
  - The restaurant syndicate's new-brand pipeline (402 new brands in 2025) as a targeted list.
  - WhatsApp-first support and demos.
  - Accountants and bookkeepers as referral partners for retail, given their reach into small shops. This is an inference with no Lebanese evidence found.
- A free trial or freemium tier, single terminal with limited features, directly counters piracy. Loyverse's free model (below) shows it can work in emerging markets.

### Gaps
- No evidence found on accountant-channel effectiveness in Lebanon, Facebook/Instagram ad performance for B2B POS, or reseller margins actually paid by Lebanese vendors.
- No BIM POS or other Lebanese vendor revenue, ARPU or Lebanese customer counts.

## 4. Lebanese/MENA SaaS startups selling to SMEs and lessons; regional expansion

### Takeaway
The best-documented examples are Lebanese-founded companies that found scale outside Lebanon. Wafeq, an accounting SaaS founded in Dubai by Lebanese Nadim Alameddine, gets 90% of its usage from Saudi Arabia. BIM POS exports via agents to the GCC and West Africa. The pattern: build in Lebanon, monetize in the GCC, Iraq and the diaspora.

### Cited Findings
- Wafeq raised a $3M seed in 2023 (Raed Ventures, Wamda Capital), when users issued 630k+ invoices a month worth $117M+. Its $7.5M Series A in Dec 2024 was led by 9900 Capital, by which time it reported 2M+ invoices and $400M a month, 90% from KSA. It cites about 4M SMEs in GCC + Egypt. — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2024/12/wamda-capital-participates-wafeq-7-5-million-series-a-round); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/wafeq-raises-3mln-seed-round-led-by-raed-ventures-and-participation-from-wamda-capital-jbsyixjv); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/start-ups/2023/01/25/uae-accounting-start-up-wafeq-raises-3m-as-it-aims-to-enter-egypt)
- Saudi e-invoicing (ZATCA) mandates drove SME software adoption in KSA. This is implied by Wafeq's KSA concentration; the direct ZATCA link is an inference. — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2024/12/wamda-capital-participates-wafeq-7-5-million-series-a-round)
- BIM POS's footprint covers the GCC, Syria and West African Lebanese-diaspora markets. — [TrustRadius](https://tr-web.dev.trustradius.com/products/bim-pos-masterdine/details)

### Inferences
- Lebanon works as a low-cost build and test market. Regulatory compliance features (e.g. ZATCA Phase 2 in KSA, Iraqi or Gulf VAT) are what unlock willingness to pay abroad.
- Sequence: Lebanon first, then the Lebanese diaspora in West Africa and Iraq/Kurdistan (Arabic, similar informality), then the GCC, where Foodics and Rewaa are entrenched and compliance is required.

### Gaps
- No Lebanon-specific SaaS pricing or traction disclosures (e.g. from Wamda, MAGNiTT, Arabnet or Executive) for SME-focused POS were found.
- No data on Syrian market re-opening (post-2024) for Lebanese vendors.

## 5. POS subscription price benchmarks (per terminal/branch per month)

### Takeaway
Benchmarks fall into three tiers:
- GCC-grade restaurant POS (Foodics) at about $100+ per branch per month.
- Global and emerging-market tools at $0–60.
- Lebanese/local list prices from about $29–40 per terminal.

For Lebanon, a $15–35 per terminal per month band, with a free or very cheap single-terminal tier, looks consistent with the evidence.

### Cited Findings
- **Foodics (KSA, MENA):**
  - Starter bundle at 423/mo monthly or 392/mo billed annually, per branch. The currency is not shown in the snippet; it is likely SAR, about $113/$105.
  - Kuwait Basic bundle at KWD 29/mo (about $94), or 27.4 annual.
  - Third parties report $53–167/mo.
  - Sources: [Foodics pricing](https://www.foodics.com/pricing); [Foodics Kuwait](https://www.foodics.com/foodics-pricing-kuw/); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/foodics/pricing)
- **Rewaa (KSA retail):** Capterra lists "$247 per user per month" and a "$349 one-time" option. This looks inconsistent and may be SAR mislabeled as USD. Verify with the vendor. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10037838/Rewaa/)
- **Loyverse (global/emerging markets):** free core POS with paid add-ons; a free version is listed. — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/retail-consumer-services-software/a/loyverse-pos/)
- **Odoo:** from about $25–31 per user per month (Capterra). — [Capterra compare](https://www.capterra.com/compare/135618-150632/Odoo-vs-Loyverse-POS)
- **Square for Restaurants Plus:** $60/mo + 2.6% + $0.10 per transaction; not usable in Lebanon. — [Voxire](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/best-pos-systems-lebanese-restaurants-2026/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/square-square-for-restaurants/pricing)
- **Lebanon local:**
  - BIM POS from $29 (contact vendor) — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/143097/BIM-POS/)
  - "Under $40 per terminal" and $99 per branch suite (vendor blog) — [Voxire](https://voxire.pages.dev/blog/pos-systems-lebanon-retail-restaurants-2026/)
  - $199 hardware bundles with free software — [OLX](https://www.olx.com.lb/q-new-po/)

### Inferences
- Suggested Lebanon price architecture, to be validated with customer interviews:
  1. **Free tier:** one terminal, basic sales and inventory, local data. Fights piracy and Loyverse.
  2. **Retail/Café plan:** about $15–20/terminal/month, or about $150–180 per year prepaid. Adds cloud sync, LBP/USD dual pricing and WhatsApp reports.
  3. **Restaurant plan:** about $25–35/terminal/month. Adds KDS, tables, delivery-app integrations and multi-branch.
  4. **Optional perpetual license:** about $250–400 plus about 20% a year for support and cloud.
- Price in USD, collect in cash, Whish or card through resellers, and offer annual prepay discounts. At about 20–30% of Foodics' price, there is a credible gap versus GCC entrants.
- Hypothetical revenue math: 2,000 paying outlets × 1.5 terminals × $20 ≈ $60k MRR (≈ $720k ARR), which shows the Lebanese market alone is modest. Regional expansion is needed for venture-scale outcomes.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch Foodics' pricing page to confirm currency and per-device terms.
- No pricing found for Marn, Qoyod POS, Daftra POS or Egyptian POS vendors.
- No independent survey of the prices Lebanese merchants actually pay.
