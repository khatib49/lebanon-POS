# POS software vendors in Lebanon: prices, features, gaps (as of Nov 2026)

**Read this first (evidence quality).** This session's network blocked direct fetching of every vendor, marketplace and review site tried (voxire.pages.dev, omegapos.com, ceevtech.com, trustradius.com, olx.com.lb all failed to resolve). Every fact below therefore comes from **web-search result summaries of the linked page, not from reading the page itself**. Numbers can be stale, misaligned or mis-summarised. The web-search budget also ran out before the last two queries (CeevTech detail; e-invoicing and Areeba), so those are gaps, not negative findings. Evidence grades used below:

- **A** = price stated on the vendor's own site/store (via search summary).
- **B** = price on a third-party listing (Capterra, SourceForge, TrustRadius, ITQlick, OLX, Facebook).
- **C** = price or claim from a competitor's blog, or a generic directory estimate.
- **None** = no price found.

"Not found" means no source surfaced; it does not mean the feature or price does not exist.

## 1. Published or quoted prices per vendor

### Takeaway
Public pricing in Lebanon is no longer rare: at least six Lebanon-targeted products publish a monthly or annual USD price (Table $10/mo, YellowPOS from $15/mo, Omega about $30/mo equivalent on annual licences, Sandooq $39/mo, RTYLR under $40/terminal/mo, MAPOS from $99/mo). The legacy vendors (BIM POS, CeevTech, POPs LEB, IDS Libra, Wizard, Socrate, SBS, Nexel, SoftPharm) still sell on quotation. A $15–35 per-till price is therefore mid-market, not the cheapest: Table and YellowPOS already sit at or below the bottom of that range.

### Inferences
- Omega's annual licences work out to about $30–32/month ($360–380 / 12; my arithmetic). That puts the best-known incumbent inside lebanon-POS's planned $15–35 band, with far more features.
- The BIM POS conflict is probably several things mixed together: a per-seat monthly tier ladder ($15/$29/$39) on listings, an older perpetual licence ($500 per user), and an ITQlick model estimate. None is confirmed for Lebanon; treat "$29/month" as a listing placeholder.
- The price floor has two anchors: $10/month self-serve (Table) and "free software" bundled with $199–699 hardware (OLX). Competing on price alone is not viable; $15–35 has to be justified by credit tabs, support and reliability.
- No set-up fee, training fee or annual-maintenance percentage was found for any Lebanese vendor. The only implementation figures are ITQlick's $500–2,000 for BIM (an estimate) and Voxire's $3,000–8,000 for Odoo.

### Inferences
- "Offline-first + USD/LBP + Arabic" is exactly what Table and Sandooq already advertise, at $10 and $39. lebanon-POS's headline cannot rest on those three alone.
- Customer credit tabs ("دين" / debt book) were not advertised by any vendor in any summary. Given how common shop credit is in Lebanese minimarkets, this looks like the strongest single differentiator to verify and then lead with.
- Mixed-tender payment with change in the other currency was not explicitly evidenced for anyone; Table's "rate snapshotted at sale time" is the closest concrete claim.
- Pharmacy is effectively closed to a generic POS: MediTrack integration and official price sync are required features, and SoftPharm claims near-total share.
- Restaurant buyers will compare against KDS, recipes, floor plans and Toters sync (MAPOS, RTYLR, ByteBite, Omega, BIM). A retail-first product should avoid leading with restaurants.

### Gaps
- Areeba or any bank card-terminal integration: no evidence for any vendor (the query could not run; budget exhausted).
- E-invoicing readiness and VAT-invoice formats: no evidence beyond RTYLR's and Wizard's "Lebanese VAT" claims — [voxire accounting blog](https://voxire.com/blog/accounting-software-lebanon-smes-2026/), [auditfirmslebanon.com](https://auditfirmslebanon.com/pos-systems/)
- Weighing-scale support: only YellowPOS's sell-by-weight article and Table's butcher page hint at it; no hardware integration detail.
- Mobile owner app: only Omega POS Mobile evidenced.
- BIM POS and CeevTech offline, dual-currency and Arabic support were not confirmed either way. Toters integration for BIM/Omega not confirmed. No Toters-published list of certified POS partners was found.
- Customer credit / accounts receivable: no evidence for or against any vendor.
